About / Merci

Perception engineer.
Robotics background.

I work in consumer robotics, developing multi-sensor perception with stereo or LiDAR as the leading observation source. My work spans geometric reasoning, model post-processing, tracking and efficient C++ implementation.

I hold an M.Sc. in Robotic Systems Engineering from RWTH Aachen University. Earlier research involved collision-engine integration and vision-based climbing analysis.
